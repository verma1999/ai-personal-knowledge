**Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** optimizes large language models by pulling facts from outside sources.

- **Search**: The system queries external knowledge bases for relevant data.
- **Fetch**: It retrieves accurate, up-to-date documents matching the user's prompt.
- **Augment**: It appends this contextual data directly into the model's prompt.
- **Generate**: The model produces a highly precise, fact-grounded response.
