Cricket is a popular bat-and-ball team sport played globally on a large outdoor field with two teams of eleven players.Facts About CricketCricket is played on a large grass field with a 22-yard pitch in the center.Two teams compete in a match, and each team has eleven players.The game uses a wooden bat, a hard leather ball, and three upright stumps called wickets.One team bats to score runs, while the other team bowls and fields to stop them.A bowler delivers the ball toward the batsman to try and get them out.Batsmen score runs by hitting the ball and running between the wickets or crossing the boundary.Umpires stand on the field to make fair decisions and enforce the rules.Test cricket is the longest format and lasts for up to five days.One Day Internationals (ODIs) limit each team to fifty overs.Twenty20 (T20) is a fast format where each team plays twenty overs.The International Cricket Council (ICC) governs world cricket.England, India, Australia, and Pakistan are major cricket-playing nations.The game began in southeast England many centuries ago.Children love to play street cricket or gully cricket in open spaces.Fielding players try to catch the ball or stop it from reaching the boundary.A batsman can get out by being bowled, caught, run out, or leg before wicket.Fans watch big tournaments on television and fill large stadiums to cheer.Great players inspire millions of children around the world to play.Playing the sport builds good teamwork, focus, and physical health.
