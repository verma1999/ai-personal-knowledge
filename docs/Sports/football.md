Football is the most popular sport in the world, played with a round ball on a large grass field by two competing teams.Facts About the GameTwo teams play against each other.Each team has eleven players.The main goal is to score points.Players kick the ball into the net.Using hands on purpose is a foul.Rules and Match PlayOnly the goalkeeper can touch the ball with hands.A standard match lasts ninety minutes.The game is split into two halves.Referees control the match and enforce rules.Out-of-bounds balls result in throw-ins.Tournaments and CultureThe FIFA World Cup is the biggest event.Millions of fans watch games on TV.Players need high speed and good stamina.Great players inspire young people globally.Professional leagues exist in many countries.Benefits and ValuesRunning helps keep players fit and healthy.The sport teaches strong teamwork.Players learn discipline on the field.Friends often play together in local parks.It brings people together across the world.
